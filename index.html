A living landscape

Before you arrive

Meet us at the water's edge.

Fernhaven is a short drive from Calapan City, tucked into the lush foothills of the Mindoro watershed.

Getting here
Take the Strong Republic Nautical Highway south from Calapan City, then follow the brown reserve signs for 12 miles. A shuttle runs from the main terminal on Fridays and Saturdays.
Nearby attractions
Local waterfalls, the provincial farm trail, and historic heritage villages are all within 30 minutes.
Reserve hours
Day visitors: 6:00 am to 8:00 pm. Guided activities meet at the Fernhaven Welcome Hut.

A thoughtful field guide

Travel well.

A little preparation helps you stay comfortable, safe, and kind to the landscape.

Weather

Expect cool mornings, warm afternoons, and quick showers. Bring layers, rain protection, sun cream, and shoes with good grip.

Safety

Stay on marked trails, check in with your guide, and carry water. Mobile coverage is limited around the eastern shore.

Care for the reserve

Keep dogs leashed, never feed wildlife, take all waste with you, and leave plants, stones, and feathers where you find them.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need previous kayaking experience?

No. Our quiet-water trip is suitable for beginners, and every guest receives a safety briefing and personal flotation device.

What should I bring on a guided walk?

Water, a light rain layer, sun protection, sturdy shoes, and your curiosity. We provide binoculars and shared field guides.

Can children join the experiences?

Children aged eight and over are welcome on the birdwatch and forest walks. Kayakers must be ten or older.
